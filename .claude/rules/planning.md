# Plan before changing

- Before changing code, configuration, documentation, rules or data, show the user a short plan and wait for their approval. The plan says what will change, which parts of the app are affected, and any decisions the user needs to make.
- Reading files, searching, checking logs and other read-only investigation are fine before the plan; use them to make the plan concrete.
- After approval, follow the plan. If the work turns out to need something the plan did not cover, stop and show the updated plan before continuing.
- Answering a question or diagnosing a problem needs no plan unless it leads to a change.
