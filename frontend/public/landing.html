How do I get started?

Choose Continue as guest to try a temporary chat, with no account needed. Create an account or log in if you want to keep your chat history.

Which files can I use?

Add Word documents, text files, or images. Images need a vision-capable model. PDF, Excel, and PowerPoint content is not currently read.

What gets saved?

Account chats are saved. Guest chats stay in this tab, with no idle timeout. Closing the tab, ending the session, or a server restart ends the chat. Files are temporary in both modes. Relevant content goes to the configured AI provider. Review important answers and keep this local app off the public internet.
